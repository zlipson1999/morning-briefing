Our fair-use policy

Flagged for review — never “convicted.”

Accusing the wrong owner is the fastest way to get a board sued. So the product is built to be careful on purpose, and we say so out loud.

The word “busted” is the brand’s bark. The evidence file is its bite — and it always shows its work.

  • ✓
    Confidence, not certaintyEvery match ships with a score and its underlying signals. Low-confidence matches are held back, never mailed.
  • ✓
    A human closes the caseOn Enforce, a reviewer verifies each violation before it reaches an owner’s mailbox.
  • ✓
    Owner’s right to replyEvery notice includes how to dispute or show a valid permit — due process, built in.
  • ✓
    Compliant data sourcingWe favor licensed and lawfully collected data, and log the provenance of every screenshot.
